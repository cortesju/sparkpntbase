The fine print.

Why is there a trial fee at all?

It covers real costs - shipping both ways, a month of corrections, and your onboarding call - and it's fully credited toward any purchase within 60 days. It also keeps the trial fleet available for people with real projects instead of tire-kickers, without us having to judge anyone.

Do I pay for the device up front?

No. You pay the $149 fee, and the device ships under a one-page agreement: SparkPNT keeps ownership during the trial, and a unit that is not returned by day 45 converts to a purchase and gets invoiced. No card holds, no large charge sitting on your statement.

What counts as "working condition"?

Working unit, complete kit (the checklist is printed inside the case lid), normal field wear absolutely fine. These units go to the field - we expect dirt.

What happens if I forget to return it?

Day 35 we nudge. Day 45 the trial converts to a purchase per the agreement - which some people choose on purpose. No collections, no surprises.

Can I trial outside the US?

Not yet - the radio units are FCC 915 MHz and export paperwork would eat the trial window. International purchases with the standard 30-day return policy work today.

Questions we get.

Do I get to keep the device?

It's a loan that renews every 12 months as long as the commitments were met (one substantial piece per quarter, one deep dive per year, FTC-compliant disclosure). Go quiet and you get a friendly off-boarding: return it, or buy it at 40% off. Most buy - that's fine by everyone.

Do you review my content before I post?

We offer a courtesy fact-check line and never require pre-approval - required review kills the authenticity that makes this worth anything. If something's technically wrong, we'll correct it publicly with kindness.

Can I trash the competition?

No. Respectful co-existence is in the agreement. Emlid and the rest make good instruments; we differentiate on openness, repairability, and price - not mud.

What does it pay?

Hardware, access, and attribution - plus 5% store credit on referral-code sales (pending final program terms). It's enthusiast-flavored on purpose. If you need a retainer, the Industry Champion track is the conversation to have.
