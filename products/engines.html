ZED-X20P vs mosaic-X5.

The chips inside the FPX and FPM, compared spec-by-spec from u-blox and Septentrio's own datasheets - written because "the FPX is the security pick" turned out to be wrong. Both engines carry OSNMA anti-spoofing. Here's what actually separates them.

The correction that triggered this report: we had been differentiating the FPX as "the security pick" on OSNMA grounds. Per the product lead and confirmed in both datasheets: the mosaic-X5 (FPM) also supports OSNMA, plus Septentrio's AIM+ interference mitigation. Security is table stakes on both - the real differences are below.

So who buys which?

Same body, same price ballpark ($1,650 vs $1,950, store-verified Aug 14), both OSNMA. The honest differentiation:
FPX · ZED-X20P

The endurance & ecosystem pick.

  • 3× lower power → 54 h battery, the longest in the family. Power budgets rule drones and remote deployments.
  • PointPerfect-native. SPARTN decoding on-chip - the tightest integration with the $15/mo Flex service we recommend for 90% of customers.
  • L-Band-ready hardware for the announced global PointPerfect service (no date - never promise one).
  • Platform security stack: secure boot, secure storage, config lockdown - matters to OEMs building products around it.
  • Smaller, cheaper, faster cold start. The volume-integration engine.
FPM · mosaic-X5

The performance-ceiling pick.

  • 100 Hz updates, <10 ms latency (vs 25 Hz) - the spec that matters for fast control loops and high-dynamics platforms.
  • AIM+ actively mitigates jamming - near jammers or in contested RF, it keeps working where detection-only receivers just report the problem.
  • Native RINEX + Ethernet + SBF - the surveyor and reference-station workflow engine; the cleanest OPUS path.
  • 0.5 ppm baseline error - half the distance-dependent error on long baselines.
  • 5 ns timing, event markers, MIL-STD vibration - the choice when the deliverable is measurements, not just navigation.
The one-line sales script: "Both are protected against spoofing - the question is whether your platform needs endurance and PointPerfect integration (FPX) or update rate, jamming immunity, and survey-grade deliverables (FPM)." For drones specifically: long-endurance mapping drone → FPX; fast autonomous platform in ugly RF, or PPK deliverables → FPM.
