USB Self Service

Plug In Your Unit.
We Will Take A Look.

Connect a SparkPNT receiver (TX2, Torch, FP series) or a SparkFun RTK unit with a USB-C cable. This page reads the unit's status, points out anything that looks wrong, gives you a simple way into its configuration menus, and prepares a report you can send to us.

  1. Power the unit on. The USB port alone does not start it on every model.
  2. Plug in a USB-C cable with data lines. Some charging cables carry power only.
  3. Pick the entry that ends in B. The unit shows two ports; USB-Enhanced-SERIAL-B is the one that talks. If the page says it picked the wrong one, choose the other.

Works in Chrome and Edge on Windows, macOS, Linux and ChromeOS. Everything runs in your browser; nothing leaves this computer until you send the report.

No unit at hand? Try a simulated TX2, a simulated FPX-T with problems or a simulated Torch on old firmware.

Drivers And What To Expect On The Cable

Every SparkPNT unit (TX2, Torch, FP series) and the SparkFun RTK Postcard and EVK talk to the computer through a WCH CH342 USB-to-serial bridge. It shows up as two ports, USB-Enhanced-SERIAL-A CH342 and USB-Enhanced-SERIAL-B CH342. Port B is the unit's console, the one this page and any terminal program use. Port A carries the GNSS receiver's own data stream on the TX2 and Postcard. Older SparkFun RTK products (Surveyor, Express, Facet) use the single-port CH340 bridge instead, shown as USB-SERIAL CH340.

Windows 10 And 11

Plug the unit in and open Device Manager, under Ports (COM & LPT). If the two CH342 entries are there, nothing to install. If you see an unknown device or a device with a warning sign, install WCH's CH343SER driver (one package for CH342 and CH343; run the setup EXE as administrator, then unplug and replug). For the older CH340 units use CH341SER. SparkFun's CH340 driver guide has screenshots.

macOS

Install WCH's CH34x VCP driver (covers CH340 through CH343; also on Homebrew as wch-ch34x-usb-serial-driver). macOS asks you to allow the driver under Privacy & Security the first time. The unit then appears as /dev/cu.wchusbserial... entries.

Linux

Recent kernels include the ch341 and ch343 modules and need no download; the unit appears as /dev/ttyACM* or /dev/ttyUSB*. Add yourself to the dialout group (log out and in again). On Ubuntu and Debian the brltty package grabs these chips; remove it if the port vanishes a second after plugging in. Older kernels take WCH's ch343ser_linux module.

ChromeOS

No driver needed. Pick the port in the browser's chooser.

Two Things That Look Like A Dead Unit But Are Not

  • The unit restarts, or stays silent, when a terminal opens the port. The bridge's DTR and RTS lines are wired to the processor's reset and boot pins (that is how firmware gets loaded). Most terminal programs assert both lines when they open the port, which holds the unit in reset: it prints nothing at all. This page releases the lines right after opening, so the unit restarts once and then talks. In a terminal program, turn off DTR and RTS (or hardware flow control) for the same result.
  • A Torch goes quiet 30 seconds after power on. The Torch cannot talk to USB and to its LoRa radio at the same time, so by default it hands the port to the radio 30 seconds after it starts (the setting is called loraSerialInteractionTimeout, under Radios). Unplug and replug the cable to get the console back, or raise the setting. This page raises it to 10 minutes while connected and puts it back when you disconnect.

Main Firmware Over USB

      
The main processor firmware (what the menus call the ESP32 firmware) can be written straight from this page over the cable. The unit restarts twice and keeps its settings. Do not unplug it while the bar moves.

      

        
        
        
        
      

      

      


      
Receiver, Radio And Tilt Firmware

      
The GNSS receiver, the LoRa radio (Torch) and the tilt sensor are updated by the unit itself over WiFi. Give it a 2.4 GHz network and this page asks the unit to fetch and install whatever is newer. The unit restarts when it is done.
